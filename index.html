Day-by-Day

Formats

In honor of International Football, we will be playing with Relegation and Promotion. Having a good day can promote you. Having a bad day can relegate you.

All Games are Scored Net

The Four Days
Day 1 Wednesday · Jan 6

Stroke Play

The Forest Country Club

How Teams Are Selected

The four Flight A players pick the teams before the trip. Each A player selects one B, one C, and one D player to complete their team.

The Game

Team stroke play. All four players' scores count toward the team total. Your score on any hole is capped at net double bogey — no blowup holes can sink the ship. Lowest combined team total wins the day.

After the Game

The Open System kicks in. The best individual net score in each of Flights B, C, and D gets promoted up one flight. The worst individual net score in each of Flights A, B, and C gets relegated down one flight. Move up or move down — you earn your spot for Day 2.

Day 2 Thursday · Jan 7

30/40 Ball

Palmira Golf & Country Club

How Teams Are Selected

After Day 1, the player relegated from Flight A, the player relegated from Flight B, the player relegated from Flight C, and the last-place Flight D player each become captains. They each pick one player from each remaining flight to form their team.

The Game

30/40 Ball — each team of 4 players must select 20 scores per 9 holes to count as their score. You must select which scores to keep after the hole is played but before you tee off on the next hole. Lowest combined team total wins the day.

After the Game

The Open System runs again. Best net score in B moves up to A. Best in C moves up to B. Best in D moves up to C. Worst in A drops to B. Worst in B drops to C. Worst in C drops to D. New flights are set for Day 3.

Day 3 Friday · Jan 8

Stableford

Old Cypress

How Teams Are Selected

Same as Day 2 — the four relegated players from Day 2 become captains and each pick their team.

The Game

Points instead of strokes. All four players earn points on every hole, and the team's points are added together. Highest team total wins. Blow up a hole? It just scores zero — no damage done.

Double bogey or worse0 pts
Bogey1 pt
Par2 pts
Birdie3 pts
Eagle4 pts

After the Game

One final round of the Open System. Flights are reshuffled the same way — best scores move up, worst scores move down. Your final flight standing after Day 3 determines which Ryder Cup team you play for on Day 4.

Day 4 Saturday · Jan 9

Ryder Cup

Colony Golf & Country Club

How Teams Are Selected

After Day 3 Stableford, Flight B standings decide the Ryder Cup captains. 1st and 4th place B players pair up as one captain team; 2nd and 3rd place B pair up as the other. Each captain pair then picks their A, C, and D players to complete their Ryder Cup roster.

The Game

Net fourball match play — head-to-head. Four matches run at the same time: A flight vs A flight, B vs B, C vs C, D vs D. Win more holes than your opponent to win your match. The team that wins the most matches across all four wins the Ryder Cup.

How It Works

Rules

Flights & Teams

The Flights

Players are divided into four flights — A, B, C, and D — based on handicap. Flight A has the best players; Flight D has the highest handicaps. There are 4 players per flight, 16 total.

The Teams

Every day, one player from each flight is grouped together to form a team: A + B + C + D. You always have one low-handicapper and one high-handicapper on your side. Teams change each day.

Who Picks Teams

DayWho Picks
Day 1The 4 Flight A players — picked before the trip
Day 2Relegated A, relegated B, relegated C + last-place D (after Day 1)
Day 3Relegated A, relegated B, relegated C + last-place D (after Day 2)
Day 4Flight B captains — set by Day 3 Stableford standings
Relegation

What Is It?

After Days 1, 2, and 3, players move up or down between flights based on how they scored that day. Play well and you move up. Play poorly and you drop down. The pressure is on every single hole.

Who Moves

ResultWhat Happens
↑ Move UpLowest net scorer in Flight B moves to A. Lowest in C moves to B. Lowest in D moves to C.
↓ Move DownHighest net scorer in Flight A drops to B. Highest in B drops to C. Highest in C drops to D.

All swaps happen at the same time. Ties are broken by scorecard countback from hole 18 (back 9, back 6, back 3, back 1). No chip-offs.

Prizes & Winning
🏆

Team Match Pot

Each member of the winning team on Days 1–3 earns their share of the daily match pot. Win the day, get paid.

🎯

Low Net of the Day

A bonus for the player with the lowest individual net score each day (Days 1–3). Ties split the bonus evenly.

📍

Closest to the Pin

Individual prize on designated par 3s. Stick it close, win the cash. Simple as that.

💰

Skins

Carry-over skins played daily. Win a skin, split the pot with your team. Skins carry until someone wins cleanly.

The overall winner is the player with the most total money across all four days. Prize amounts confirmed before the trip.
