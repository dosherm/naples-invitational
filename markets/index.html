How it works. Everyone starts with B$1,000. Each share of an answer pays B$1 if that answer wins, so a price is the group's odds: 62¢ means 62%. Buying an answer pushes its price up. When a market's question is decided, the person who made it (or the organizer) marks the answer and the board pays out. Bogey Bucks are points for bragging rights: nothing here takes, holds or pays real money.
